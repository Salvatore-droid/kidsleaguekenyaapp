Install as a web app
1
Open in Chrome
Make sure you're visiting this page in Google Chrome on your Android device.
2
Tap the install banner or menu
Chrome will show an "Add to Home Screen" banner at the bottom — tap it. Or tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right and choose Add to Home screen.
Chrome menu → Add to Home screen
3
Confirm and launch
Tap Add in the dialog. The KLK icon will appear on your home screen and launch like a native app.
💡 If an "Install App" button appears in the header above, tap it for a one-click install — no menu needed.
1
Open in Safari
This page must be open in Safari on your iPhone or iPad. Chrome and other iOS browsers can't install apps.
2
Tap the Share button
At the bottom of Safari, tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up).
Share button at the bottom of Safari
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
Add to Home Screen → Add
4
Launch from your home screen
Find the KLK icon on your home screen. It opens full-screen with no browser bar.
⚠️ Apple requires Safari for installation. If you're in another browser, copy the URL and paste it into Safari first.
